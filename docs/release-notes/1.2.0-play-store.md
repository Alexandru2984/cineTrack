# Play Console release notes — 1.2.0 (versionCode 18)

Play allows 500 characters per language in "What's new". Both entries below are
within that. Paste them into the matching language on the release.

Written for someone who uses the app, not someone who reads the changelog: it
says what changed for them, in the order they will notice it.

---

## ro-RO (368 characters)

```
Mesaje criptate cap-coadă. Când ambele conturi au criptarea activată, Văzute stochează mesajele fără să le poată citi, iar numărul de siguranță îți confirmă că nimeni nu stă între voi.

Interfață completă în română.
Blocare și raportare pentru conturi, liste și mesaje.
Up Next nu mai propune episoade care încă nu au apărut.
Corecții de accesibilitate și contrast.
```

## en-US (352 characters)

```
End-to-end encrypted messages. Once both accounts turn encryption on, Văzute stores your messages without being able to read them, and a safety number confirms nobody is sitting between you.

Full Romanian and English interfaces.
Blocking and reporting for accounts, lists and messages.
Up Next no longer offers episodes that have not aired.
Accessibility and contrast fixes.
```

## What was deliberately left out

**The terms re-acceptance.** Everyone is asked to accept the updated Terms on
their next visit. That belongs in the app, where it is unavoidable, not in a
release note nobody reads before updating.

**Everything under "Security" in the changelog.** Access-token revocation, the
metrics bearer token, server-side blocking enforcement, moderation grants —
real work, and none of it a sentence a viewer can act on. Listing hardening in
a store note reads as reassurance, and reassurance is not what the field is for.

**The Expo SDK bump and CI changes.** Nothing a user experiences.

## Data safety declaration

Encryption changes what the Play data-safety form should say about messages:
they are still collected, but the service can no longer read them once both
sides have keys. If the current declaration claims message content is
accessible for moderation, it now needs the qualifier that a reported message
is disclosed *by the reporter*. Worth checking before this release goes to
production track.
